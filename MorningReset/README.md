MorningReset

MorningReset is a minimal morning flow that helps you start your day with direction — not distraction.

Most people reach for their phone in the morning and immediately lose control of how the day begins.
MorningReset replaces that moment with a short, structured reset.

What it does

Instead of feeding you content, MorningReset guides you through a simple flow:

a short yes/no check-in
a clear intention
a daily mode (Protect / Steady / Push)
a short observation based on your state
one grounded action to begin the day

You don’t scroll.
You don’t consume.
You start.

Philosophy

No motivation.
No pressure.
No endless content.

Just a calm, honest reset.

MorningReset is not designed to make you feel better.
It helps you decide how to begin.

Core Modes
Protect → low energy, reduce pressure, stabilize
Steady → maintain rhythm, stay consistent
Push → use momentum, move forward

Each mode changes how the app responds — including actions, tone, and guidance.

Premium

Premium adds depth — not noise.

pattern-based insights from recent days
guided pause moments
extended action flows (breath, mobility, physical reset)

Only shown when relevant. Never forced.

Action Layer

MorningReset focuses on small, real actions:

drink water
slow breathing
light movement
short mobility flows

The goal is simple:
shift your state before the day takes over.

Ritual Layer

Each week introduces a simple line to stay with — not to repeat mechanically, but to carry through the day.

No affirmations.
No forced positivity.
Just a consistent mental anchor.

What it is not
not a content feed
not a meditation library
not a productivity tracker

MorningReset does not try to do everything.
It protects the first minutes of your day.

Tech

Built with SwiftUI using a state-driven architecture.
Local-first. No accounts. No tracking.
