# Morning Reset Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 15, 2026

Morning Reset is built to use as little personal data as possible. The launch build does not use third-party analytics, ad SDKs, accounts, or cross-app tracking.

## What stays on your device

- Your morning flow progress, streaks, answers, and flow check-out history
- Your selected intention and other app preferences
- Premium entitlement state and paywall timing

## What Apple handles

- App Store purchases, free trials, renewals, billing, and restore purchases

## External requests the app can make

- App Store and StoreKit requests needed for subscription purchase and restore
- Optional on-device insight generation on supported Apple devices

Morning Reset 1.0 does not require a separate account to use Premium or the core ritual.

## What Morning Reset does not do

- No third-party advertising
- No selling personal data
- No cross-app tracking
- No account creation in the launch build
- No hidden premium features beyond what the paywall lists

## Your choices

- Use the core Morning Reset flow without Premium
- Manage or cancel your subscription in App Store settings
- Restore a previous subscription from the paywall
