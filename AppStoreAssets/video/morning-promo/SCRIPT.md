# SCRIPT — morning-promo

**Voice:** `af_heart` (Kokoro, local) — the same voice the app itself speaks with, at the same rendering settings as `scripts/build_voice.py`.
**Voice settings:** speed 0.78, played at rate 0.8 — unhurried, pitch preserved.
**Voice direction:** Someone sitting on the edge of the bed, not an announcer. Every line lands and then stops; the silence after it is part of the line. Never bright, never selling.

---

## Line 1 — (Frame 1, no narration)

**Time:** 0.0 – 4.5s
**Delivery:** None. The app's own melody carries this frame alone.

## Line 2 — The turn (Frame 2)

**Time:** 4.5 – 9.5s
**Delivery:** Plain and low. The emphasis sits on *one*.

    Your alarm asks one thing of you this morning.

## Line 3 — The payoff (Frame 3)

**Time:** 9.5 – 14.5s
**Delivery:** Two sentences with a real pause between them. The second is almost an aside.

    A smile. And the bell lets go.

## Line 4 — The offer (Frame 4)

**Time:** 14.5 – 20.0s
**Delivery:** Warm, unhurried. This is an invitation, not a feature.

    Your practice is here whenever you want it.

## Line 5 — What is inside (Frame 5)

**Time:** 20.0 – 25.5s
**Delivery:** Three clauses, each with its own beat, each landing as its screen arrives.

    Your breath. Then your pulse. And a month of your own mornings.

## Line 6 — The name (Frame 6)

**Time:** 25.5 – 29.0s
**Delivery:** Quiet. Say it the way you would say it to one person.

    Inner Light. Daily practice.
