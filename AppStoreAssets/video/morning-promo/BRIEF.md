## Intent

A promo for **Inner Light**, an iOS app whose showcase is its alarm: at the hour
you choose the phone plays a melody, and what it opens into is a morning rather
than a snooze button. The video has to dramatise the real flow as the app now
performs it — the melody, the bell drawing away, one smile, and then the
practice *offered* rather than demanded.

Tone: dawn-quiet, unhurried, 35mm. Nobody performs happiness at the camera. The
piece should feel like the thing it is selling — if the cut is busy, it is
lying.

The hook is the opposite of every other alarm ad: this one asks for almost
nothing. The whole selling point is the lightness.

## Customizations

- Generated footage from HeyGen for the human shots; real app screens as inserts. Screens inside generated shots stay dim and unreadable so no interface is invented — only the real captures show UI.
- Narration in the app's own voice: Kokoro `af_heart`, the same voice the app speaks with. The promo should sound like the product.
- One continuous morning, not a montage of features. If it must be cut, each shot continues the last.

## Notes

- Copy law, non-negotiable (the app's own): name what is present, never what is absent. No "stop hitting snooze", no "tired of mornings". Never promise an effect — describe the experience. This is a Health & Fitness app and effect claims are an App Review risk as well as a lie.
- Retired words: miss, lose, break, fail, behind, catch up, fix, stuck, struggle, stress, should, must, need to, learn, teach, master, beginner.
- No countdowns, no urgency, no invented testimonials or star ratings.
- The pulse reading is a wellness reading, never a medical measurement — if a number appears on screen it carries no claim.
