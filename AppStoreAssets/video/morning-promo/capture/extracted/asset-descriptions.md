# Asset inventory

No site was captured — the product is an iOS app, and its own screens are the
source of truth. These are real captures from the shipping build, not mockups.

- `capture/assets/00_SetAlarm.png` — the alarm setup screen. Weekday and weekend
  time cards, the line saying when it next rings, and the confirmation that it
  comes through Silent mode and Sleep Focus. Dawn-mist background, periwinkle
  accent, serif headline.
- `capture/assets/01_Today.png` — the Today screen. The energy orb, the primary
  practice invitation, and the morning ritual entry beneath it.
- `capture/assets/04_Practice.png` — the guided practice player. Breathing ring,
  the current step, and the timer.

Generated footage (HeyGen) supplies every human shot. Its house style is fixed
in `../heygen/ai_shots.json` and must be reused verbatim: 35mm vertical, natural
light, muted periwinkle and slate, one person alone, camera barely moving, any
phone in shot dim and unreadable.
