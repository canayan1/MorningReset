# Morning Reset — background music prompts

For Suno / Udio / ElevenLabs Music etc. One consistent track across all content = a recognizable
brand sound. Generate **instrumental, loopable, calm**. Matches the app's own ambient (warm pad,
soft struck bells, airy flute).

## Sonic signature (one line — reuse everywhere)
> Warm, weightless morning ambient — soft analog pad, gentle struck bells, airy wooden flute,
> light breath textures, sunrise warmth. Instrumental, no vocals.

## ⭐ Primary prompt (versatile bed for most posts)
> Calm cinematic wellness ambient for a sunrise morning ritual. A warm analog synth pad as the bed,
> soft struck bells and kalimba accents, a distant airy bamboo/wooden flute melody, gentle
> fingerpicked nylon guitar or harp, subtle breath-like textures and soft tape warmth. Spacious
> reverb, organic and acoustic, hopeful and serene — like first light through the curtains. Slow
> gentle pulse around 75 BPM, warm major key (A or D major). Smoothly loopable, builds very gently,
> never busy. Fully instrumental.

## Variation A — Morning bright (app Reels + energy carousels)
> Same warm sunrise ambient, slightly brighter and more uplifting: add a light shaker and soft mallet
> pulse around 80 BPM, a gentle sense of forward motion, optimistic and energizing but still calm.
> Instrumental, no vocals.

## Variation B — Night wind-down (sleep / sound & breath content)
> Same palette, slower ~58 BPM, darker and softer. Lead with a low warm pad, single struck
> temple-bell tones ringing out and fading, a soft humming drone (like a gentle Om), minimal and
> spacious — for winding down into sleep. Instrumental, no vocals.

## Exclude / negative prompt
> no vocals, no lyrics, no heavy drums, no EDM, no aggressive bass, no sudden dynamics,
> no sad minor-key, no sappy piano-ballad cliché, no busy arrangement.

## Settings & tips
- **Instrumental: ON.** (Suno: tick Instrumental or add `[instrumental]`. Udio: leave lyrics empty.)
- Generate ~60s, then **loop a clean 15–20s section** (most Reels are 15–30s).
- Keep the **first ~0.5s clean** so the Reel hook lands; let it fade in.
- Export highest quality (WAV/320kbps).
- **Pick ONE track and reuse it** across content for brand recognition; use the bright/night
  variations only when the mood demands it.
- **Commercial rights:** generate on a **paid plan** (Suno Pro/Premier, Udio Standard+, ElevenLabs)
  so the track is cleared for ads/promos. Avoid free tiers that restrict commercial use.
- **Mixing in the Reel:** sit the music low (background); if you add a voiceover later, duck music
  to ~-15 dB under it. For app-promo Reels, either mute the app audio or let the music be the only
  audio track.
