# Energy Reset — audit against the 2.0 vision (19 Aug 2026)

Vision: *an all-day school of energy practice — 10 schools, 250 routines, teachings-led,
optional check-in, tiered unlocks.* This audit lists where the app still contradicts that.

---

## P0 — The app is two products bolted together

**1. ✅ FIXED — The main CTA doesn't use the schools at all.**
"Start today's reset" → `startFlow()` → `resolveQuiz(.steady)` → `weeklyAffirmation` →
`morningSound` → `results` → `insightPreview` → `guidedPause` → `action` → `win` → `flowCheckout`.
That's the **old 8-screen morning ritual**. The 250 routines live in a side tab.
→ *The primary daily loop should be: today's routine from your school.*

**2. ✅ FIXED — Library tab duplicates Schools.**
`LibraryView` lists old `EnergyPath` practices (reiki/breathwork/qigong). Now that Schools
exists with 250 routines, there are two competing content systems in the tab bar.

**3. ✅ FIXED — Wins tab is the old "First Win" habit**, unrelated to school/routine progress.

**4. ✅ FIXED — The paywall sells the wrong product.**
It offers "Rise & Flow", "weekly pattern insight", "a guided pause" — it never mentions
ten schools, 250 routines, or the Foundations/Deep tiers. This is the conversion surface.

**5. ✅ FIXED — Onboarding contradicts itself.**
The user now picks a school (e.g. *Sleep & Rest*), but the next screens still say
"Matching you with **Reiki**…" / "We matched you with **Reiki**" because `building` and
`plan` still run on the old 3-path `EnergyPath` model.

---

## P1 — Vision gaps

**6. Content is English-only; the app ships EN/TR/ES.** Turkish and Spanish users get
English teachings and routines. Either localize the content or gate languages honestly.

**7. "Reset your energy in 30 days" is a promise with no mechanism.** There is no 30-day
arc, no day counter, no sense of progression — only a completion tally per school.

**8. Old screens still ship** and now read as clutter: `morningPages`, `monthlyStory`,
`breathReset`, `mobilityFlow`, `cycleComplete`, `pathLearn`, `firstWinPick`.

**9. Widget** still shows the old ritual streak/mantra, not school practice.

**10. Design rule tension (accepted, flag for decision).** Teaching and routine screens are
inherently text-heavy vs the ≤20% rule. Currently mitigated with progressive disclosure
(teachings collapsed, sources collapsed, one step at a time in the player).

---

## P2 — Store / ops

**11. Tier prices missing in App Store Connect** — Foundations and Deep are `MISSING_METADATA`.
Purchases cannot complete until priced (needs the web UI).
**12. App Store screenshots** are still pre-schools.
**13. Version is 1.2 (build 4)** — needs 2.0 for this scope.
**14. No intro offer on the tiers** (annual All-Access has a 7-day trial; tiers have none).

---

## Recommended order

1. **Rewire the daily loop** to the schools (P0-1) — biggest coherence win
2. **Fix onboarding continuity** (P0-5) — it's actively wrong today
3. **Rewrite the paywall** around schools/tiers (P0-4) — the money surface
4. **Resolve Library + Wins** (P0-2/3) — remove duplication
5. Retire dead screens (P1-8), add a 30-day arc (P1-7)
6. Store: prices, screenshots, 2.0 bump, submit (P2)


---

## Fixed on 19 Aug (same day)

- **P0-1** Primary CTA is now **"Start today's practice"** → opens today's routine from your
  school in the guided player. The old 8-screen ritual moved to the "…" menu as
  *Classic guided reset* (kept, not deleted). Tests rewired to the menu entry.
- **P0-4** Paywall now sells the real product: *"Ten schools. 250 routines."*, one free
  routine per school forever, All-Access vs single tier from $4.99/mo. All three contexts
  (onboarding / contextual / periodic) rewritten in EN/TR/ES.
- **P0-5** Onboarding is continuous: building + plan now name **the school you picked**
  ("You're starting with Sleep & Rest"), its colour, symbol, tagline and routine count.

Full suite green after each change.

- **P0-2** Library is now a **Practice Library**: all 250 routines across the ten schools,
  searchable by name/purpose/school and filterable by length (≤5 / 6–15 / 15+), with free
  and locked states. The old EnergyPath practice list is gone — no more duplicate systems.
- **P0-3** Wins now **leads with practice progress**: day streak, routines completed, schools
  touched, and a per-school breakdown. The First Win habit stays below it.
