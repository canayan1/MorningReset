Inner Light

Ten traditions.
One practice a day.

Most wellness apps hand you a timer and a voice. Inner Light teaches the practice first — what it is, where it comes from, and how to do it — then guides you through it, step by step.

The ten schools

  • Reiki — the hands-on Japanese practice
  • Pranayama — the breath practices
  • Qigong — slow movement and breath
  • Bhavana — cultivating the mind
  • Hatha — postures and long holds
  • Nada Yoga — sound and listening
  • Löyly — heat, cold and the swing between
  • Nidra — sleep and deep rest
  • Shinrin-yoku — light, air and slow attention
  • Notes to Self — the Stoic journal, brought up to date

Your energy orb

Every practice you finish feeds a small orb of light. It grows brighter and bigger the longer you keep going — a streak you can actually see. Miss a day and nothing is lost.

Honest by design

Traditional practices are labelled as traditional. Evidence-based schools cite their sources inside the app. Routines involving cold, heat, breathwork or movement carry safety notes. Nothing here treats or cures anything.

Private by design

No account, no sign-up, no analytics, no tracking. The optional check-in reads a selfie entirely on your device and never saves it. Your practice history stays on your phone.

Free forever: one routine in every school — ten in total — plus the daily loop and the optional check-in.
