Morning Reset Support

Morning Reset partners with the moment you open your eyes. From the night you set your wake time, it waits on your lock screen via a Live Activity so it is the first thing you see when you reach for your phone.

How Morning Reset reaches you

  • Lock-screen Live Activity (iOS 16.1+): a persistent Morning Reset card stays on your lock screen from bedtime to your wake time. Tap it the moment you open your eyes.
  • Local notification backup: at your scheduled wake time, a notification also fires so you have a second touchpoint.
  • Morning Reset does not replace your trusted alarm. Keep using the iOS Clock alarm to wake up.
  • If your phone is in Silent or Focus mode, the notification may be quiet, but the Live Activity is still visible on your lock screen.
  • The core morning ritual works without Premium.
